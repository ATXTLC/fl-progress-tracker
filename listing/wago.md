# Wago Addons listing draft — FL Progress Tracker

**Name:** FL Progress Tracker · **Game version:** WoW Forever (16001) · **License:** MIT · **Icon:** icon_400.png

**Short description:** Read-only progress tracker: saves your characters' quests, gear, professions and guild locally for an offline dashboard.

**Description:**
FL Progress Tracker records what your client already shows you — level, XP, zone, gear, quest log, completed quests, professions, guild, achievements — into its SavedVariables file on `/reload` or logout. No automation, no network calls, no ads, no donation prompts; it's free.

Your data never leaves your PC unless you upload it yourself at https://www.foreverledger.net/ (drag the file onto the Account page, or use the optional companion uploader). There you can browse quests by zone/class/status, maps, gear and guild progress.

Commands: `/fl` (help), `/fl errors`, `/fl health`, `/fl watch <id>`, `/fl leaderboard on|off` (off by default), `/fl link CODE`.

Install with the Wago app / WowUp, or extract the zip to `_forever_\Interface\AddOns\` so the path is `AddOns\fl-progress-tracker\fl-progress-tracker.toc`.
